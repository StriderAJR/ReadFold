Language-learning reading application

ReadFold

From guided reading to fluent reading. ReadFold helps language learners read original-language texts with contextual explanations instead of switching constantly between a book, dictionary, and grammar guide.

What is ReadFold?

ReadFold is a web-based reading and language-learning project, initially focused on Japanese. It is designed to make authentic texts easier to understand, while helping learners become progressively less dependent on reading assistance.

Readers will be able to explore sentences with optional pronunciation guides (furigana, hiragana, and romaji), literal and natural translations, word meanings, kanji explanations, and grammar notes.

Development status: ReadFold is currently in development. This page describes the product's intended capabilities, not a currently available public release. The application is not yet open for general registration.

Planned features

Guided Japanese reading

Read sentences in context, reveal translations and pronunciation aids as needed, and explore individual words and kanji.

Vocabulary and grammar learning

Review contextual word definitions, grammar explanations, and examples encountered in reading material.

Reading progress

Track reading position, learned vocabulary, kanji, grammar, and practice results.

Optional account synchronization

Planned Google and GitHub sign-in will let readers connect their learning data to an account and synchronize progress between devices. Local browser storage is also planned.

Accounts and privacy

When account functionality is released, ReadFold intends to use Google or GitHub sign-in only for authentication and account identification. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or other private Google services.

ReadFold is an independent project and is not affiliated with Google, GitHub, or Supabase. Learn more in the ReadFold Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
