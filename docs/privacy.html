ReadFold Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ReadFold, an independent language-learning reading application, handles information in its current website and in its planned account-enabled application. ReadFold is currently under development. Functionality described as planned does not collect data until it is implemented and enabled.

1. Who operates ReadFold and how to contact us

ReadFold is an independent project maintained by the developer of the ReadFold GitHub repository. Questions about privacy, access, correction, or deletion can be submitted through the project issue tracker. GitHub issues are public and may require a GitHub account; do not include passwords, authentication tokens, or sensitive information in a public issue.

2. Information we collect and its sources

Account information (when sign-in is enabled)

ReadFold plans to support sign-in through Google and GitHub, using Supabase Auth. With your permission, the identity provider may supply a provider-specific account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image. ReadFold uses this information to recognize your account, create or link a ReadFold profile, and maintain an authenticated session.

ReadFold's planned Google sign-in uses standard identity scopes such as openid, email, and profile. ReadFold does not request permission to read Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google contacts, or Google Calendar.

Learning information (when reading features are enabled)

ReadFold plans to process reading position and history, saved vocabulary, kanji and grammar learning states, quiz results, display preferences, and synchronization identifiers. These are created through your use of the application and are needed to provide the reading and learning experience.

Local browser information

When implemented, ReadFold will store reading progress, preferences, and a queue of pending synchronization changes locally in browser storage such as IndexedDB. Local diagnostic information may also be retained in the browser. Clearing site data can remove this local information.

Website hosting information

The current public informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser and request details, to deliver the website and protect its infrastructure under GitHub's own privacy terms. ReadFold does not currently embed its own advertising trackers or analytics scripts on these informational pages.

3. Why we use information

  • To authenticate users and maintain account sessions.
  • To save reading preferences and learning progress.
  • To synchronize account-associated learning data between devices when enabled.
  • To support troubleshooting and respond to requests you submit.
  • To protect accounts and prevent unauthorized access or misuse.

ReadFold does not sell personal information and does not use Google profile data for targeted advertising.

4. Services that process information

Google and GitHub: provide optional identity sign-in. They process information according to their own privacy policies.

Supabase: is the planned authentication and database infrastructure provider. When account features are enabled, authentication information and synchronized learning data may be processed and stored through Supabase.

GitHub Pages: hosts the public website and may host public application assets and reading content. Public reading content does not require sharing your private learning history with GitHub.

ReadFold does not intentionally share account or learning data with advertisers or data brokers. Data may be disclosed where required by applicable law.

5. Where data is stored and how it is protected

Local learning information is planned to be stored on your device in browser storage. Account and synchronized learning information will be stored through Supabase when those features are enabled. ReadFold intends to use authenticated access controls and per-user database restrictions. Communication with hosted services uses HTTPS. No online storage system can guarantee complete security.

ReadFold plans to keep diagnostic logs on the device rather than upload them automatically. A diagnostic report would only be shared with the developer if you deliberately export and send it.

6. How long information is kept

Browser data generally remains until you clear website storage, the browser removes it, or an implemented application control deletes it. Account data and synchronized learning records are intended to be retained while the account remains active or until a valid deletion request is completed, subject to technical backup cycles and legal requirements. ReadFold does not currently publish a fixed backup deletion interval; this policy will be updated before public launch to reflect actual retention settings.

7. Your choices, access, and deletion

You may decline Google or GitHub sign-in; account-dependent features may then be unavailable. You can revoke a provider's authorization from your Google or GitHub account settings. Revoking access does not itself erase data already stored by ReadFold.

You can remove local browser data using your browser's site-data settings. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of cloud account information once accounts are enabled, contact the developer through the project issue tracker without publishing private credentials or sensitive personal data. The developer may need to verify account ownership before processing a request. Deleting local browser data does not automatically delete cloud records.

8. Children

ReadFold is not directed specifically at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.

9. International processing

Third-party providers may process data in countries different from your own. Their data-processing terms and the location of the deployed service may affect where information is stored or processed.

10. Updates to this policy

This policy may change as ReadFold is developed. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. The privacy policy will be reviewed again before account features are publicly released.
