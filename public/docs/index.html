Language learning through reading

From guided reading to fluent reading.

ReadFold is an experimental language-learning application that helps readers understand original-language books through translations, vocabulary, grammar explanations, and reading progress tracking.

Currently in development

ReadFold is not yet publicly available. Planned features include sentence-by-sentence reading assistance, kanji and grammar analysis, personalized learning progress, and offline reading.

Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details about the planned service and how data is handled when features become available.

Questions or privacy requests: contact the developer on GitHub.
